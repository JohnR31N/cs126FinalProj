# Two changes to Gaussian-weighted pretraining

This exploratory follow-up reuses the marked MNIST experiment, its known pixel and pretraining-only Gaussian baselines, the same training indices and the same five seeds. The primary comparison is held-out marked accuracy against the original pretraining-only Gaussian. Also measure clean accuracy and assignment flips on redraws of the background. Compare within seed and report the sample standard deviation. Use no test results to choose strengths, widths or checkpoints.

Test two changes independently. In the first, average the mean-one Gaussian with a uniform map, giving every pixel a weight of at least 0.5. In the second, estimate a center for each observed training image by taking the intensity centroid after multiplication by the original central Gaussian. This limits the influence of faraway bright marks on the centroid. Center a new Gaussian at that point; a blank input defaults to the image center. Keep sigma at seven pixels and normalize each map to mean one. Both modifications apply only during the 100 pretraining epochs. Then use ordinary pixel IDEC for 5,600 updates with ten clusters. The training code sees only the observed input. Use the generator's clean images solely to audit average weights on handwriting and added symbols after constructing the maps.
