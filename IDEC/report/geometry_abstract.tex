\textbf{Abstract.} We reproduced IDEC at 88.10\% MNIST accuracy (published 88.06\%). We then placed a geometric mark in blank space beside each digit. Moving the mark changed 6.20\% of cluster assignments while leaving all handwriting intact. Background augmentation reduced this to 4.34\% across five paired seeds. With the same augmented inputs, reconstructing pixels shared across views yielded 4.18\% flips. A Gaussian center prior yielded 4.16\% flips and the highest mean marked-image accuracy (72.91\%).
