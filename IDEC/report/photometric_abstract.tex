\textbf{Abstract.} We reproduced IDEC at 88.10\% MNIST accuracy (published 88.06\%) and tested whether a model benefits from reconstructing task-irrelevant background. Across 60 fits we compared whole-image brightness/contrast jitter, rotation and translation with a Gaussian reconstruction prior. Whole-image translation achieved 80.63\% accuracy on marked digits, versus 71.42\% with Gaussian weighting. Moving only the decoration switched 3.41\% and 5.19\% of assignments, respectively. Clean-only controls and a held-out shape probe distinguish accuracy gains, assignment sensitivity and accessible background information.
