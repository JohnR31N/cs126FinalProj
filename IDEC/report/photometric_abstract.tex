\textbf{Abstract.} We reproduced IDEC at 88.10\% MNIST accuracy (published 88.06\%). Across 105 controlled fits, we studied task-irrelevant geometric backgrounds, whole-image augmentation and a Gaussian reconstruction prior. In the random-background study, ordinary IDEC fell from 73.96\% clean accuracy to 65.40\% on marked digits. With the marked inputs fixed, a clean reconstruction target recovered accuracy to 71.44\% and reduced switches when the background changed. Whole-image translation reached 77.63\%. Stage ablations, matched-energy backgrounds and mark-free controls distinguish effects on clustering from changes in reconstruction.
