\textbf{Abstract.} A PyTorch reproduction of IDEC with the authors' initialization reached 88.10\% transductive MNIST clustering accuracy (published 88.06\%). We then assigned horizontal or vertical stripes independently of digit identity and fitted new models on training data only. Under strong stripes, standard IDEC changed cluster on 41.72\% of test digits when the same image switched orientation. Removing the orientation contrast from the reconstruction penalty reduced this rate to 39.50\% and raised mean digit accuracy from 58.79\% to 59.43\%. All five paired seeds became less sensitive to orientation; four gained accuracy. Shared pretrained features already exhibited substantial sensitivity. The results localize a modest contribution to the final-stage reconstruction objective.
