\textbf{Abstract.} We reproduced IDEC at 88.10\% transductive MNIST clustering accuracy (published 88.06\%). We then added random horizontal or vertical stripes and tested whether the same held-out digit changed cluster when its stripe direction switched. Under strong stripes, removing the orientation contrast from the fine-tuning loss lowered the mean flip rate from 41.72\% to 39.50\%. A follow-up crossed the pretraining and fine-tuning losses. With projected fine-tuning held fixed, changing pretraining reduced flips from 39.53\% to 2.61\% across five paired seeds. Mean digit accuracy rose from 59.96\% to 69.61\%, with one seed losing accuracy. For this known background pattern, the choice of reconstruction loss mattered most during autoencoder pretraining.
