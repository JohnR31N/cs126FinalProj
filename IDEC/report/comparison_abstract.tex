\textbf{Abstract.} We reproduced IDEC at 88.10\% MNIST clustering accuracy (published 88.06\%). Adding two background symbols reduced held-out accuracy by 6.77 percentage points for DEC and 8.09 for IDEC. IDEC lost more in four of five seeds, with a paired difference of $1.32\pm3.35$ points. A Gaussian reconstruction prior applied in both stages reduced IDEC's drop to 4.44 points, largely through lower clean accuracy. Applying the prior only in pretraining raised marked accuracy from 65.14\% to 68.80\%. In a separate diagnostic, changing only the reconstruction targets to clean digits improved marked accuracy by 6.04 points.
