On clean data, standard IDEC scored $73.27\%$ held-out ACC and projected reconstruction scored $73.16\%$. Both had exactly zero orientation flips, as the two test inputs are identical at amplitude zero. Removing all reconstruction gave $77.37\%$ clean ACC. This is a paired ablation of our final-stage objective after ordinary AE pretraining.

At amplitude 0.3, digit ACC was $71.75\%$ for standard IDEC and $72.50\%$ for the projection. Their flip rates were $3.76\%$ and $3.27\%$. The clustering-only model scored $73.57\%$ ACC but switched clusters on $5.50\%$ of paired images. Which objective looks preferable therefore depends on whether the question concerns digit identity, stability under a background change, or both.
