# Per-image Gaussian center with cluster saliency

For each observed training image, compute its intensity centroid under the original central Gaussian. Form a new Gaussian of width seven pixels at that image's centroid. Multiply it by 0.5 plus the image's frozen cluster saliency (scaled to zero through one) and normalize to mean one. Apply these weights during the 100-epoch pretraining stage. Use ordinary IDEC for 5,600 fine-tuning updates. The clean training branch uses clean images and a teacher trained only on clean digits. The marked branch uses the corresponding marked images and teacher. Reuse the archived saliency maps and donor indices from revision 187fd7c. The shuffled control keeps each image's own center and substitutes another image's saliency.

Use the original split and five paired seeds. The primary evaluation trains only on clean digits and adds symbols at test time. Also score clean-to-clean and marked-to-marked accuracy, and assignment flips when the same background symbols are redrawn. Compare the new combination against adaptive center alone, fixed Gaussian with aligned saliency, and adaptive center with shuffled saliency. Estimate the center-by-saliency interaction within seed by subtracting the saliency gain for fixed centers from the saliency gain for adaptive centers. The earlier control outcomes and test split were already known. Digit labels and generator masks are reserved for evaluation.
