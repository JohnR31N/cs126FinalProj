# Conditional whole-image centering at inference

The preceding Gaussian-shape experiment exposed a large loss after moving an entire marked image three pixels. This observation motivated a separate prospective test. Keep the adaptive circular Gaussian × aligned saliency reconstruction method and ordinary IDEC fine-tuning. Test a single inference-time correction on different images and marks. The hypothesis is that restoring unusual image positions improves clustering under a rigid shift with little change to naturally centered inputs.

1. Calibrate using only the 12,000 clean fitting images. Compute their existing width-seven, intensity-weighted centroids. Use the coordinate-wise median as the reference position. Use the 99th percentile of Euclidean distances to that position as the threshold. These two calibration quantities are shared by all fits.
2. On an observed image beyond the threshold, round the vector towards the reference to integer pixels. Cap each component at three pixels and at the legal bounds that preserve every nonzero observed pixel. Apply this *single* translation to the entire image. Leave the image in place inside the threshold. No saliency teacher, foreground segmentation, generator labels, offsets or clean counterpart are used at inference.
3. Take the next 10,000 MNIST training-pool IDs (permutation positions 22,000 through 31,999, zero-based) beyond the fitting set and the first shape holdout. Draw new detached symbols with location seed 81129 and attribute seed 91129. Assign the whole-image three-pixel shift using seed 101129, retaining every pair for which its assigned translation fits. Previous coursework used clean digits from this pool, but these marked/shifted views and their scores have not been examined for this test.
4. Refit the incumbent with its archived clean teacher maps at paired student seeds 0–4. For each frozen student and the same training-derived digit mapping, compare raw and conditionally centered inputs. Score clean and marked views on the full 10,000 and on the shift-eligible subset, before and after the generator shift. As an evaluation-only check, undo the known test shift and verify exact restoration of both centered pixel arrays. Its cluster accuracy equals the corresponding raw centered-subset accuracy by construction.
5. Primary endpoint is the within-model change in shifted marked-digit accuracy. Support the inference option for position shifts if it improves this endpoint in at least four seeds, and if mean clean and marked accuracy on the full centered holdout each decline by no more than one percentage point. Report all paired changes, means, sample SDs, intervention rates, and the unshifted comparisons. The original benchmark remains the reference for the reconstruction objective. This position test does not identify a separate effect of reconstruction on background memorization.
