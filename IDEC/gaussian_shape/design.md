# Does the shape of the Gaussian matter?

This follow-up starts from the released adaptive-center Gaussian times aligned cluster saliency. Moving a circular window added 0.09 percentage points over the fixed window with the same saliency on the previously inspected official test. We hypothesize that changing the footprint of the window to match the observed stroke distribution can improve resistance to detached symbols. Freeze the following choices before fitting any new students.

## One change to the weighting rule

Keep the existing saliency multiplier, per-image mean normalization, student architecture, initialization seeds, training schedule and inference. For each clean training image, use the same center as the circular method: the intensity centroid under a fixed width-seven Gaussian. Use these same masses to compute a two-dimensional spatial covariance about that center. Add one square pixel to each diagonal for numerical stability. Let the major-to-minor Gaussian standard-deviation ratio be the fourth root of the ratio of the two regularized covariance eigenvalues, capped at two. For ratio r, the major and minor Gaussian variances are 49r and 49/r. This holds the continuous Gaussian area constant and shrinks the estimated elongation toward a circle. A blank or isotropic image uses a circle.

Refit four arms in the same run with aligned saliency: fixed circle, released adaptive circle, adaptive ellipse following the major axis, and the same ellipse rotated 90 degrees about the same center. The last arm isolates direction at identical elongation. Student seeds 0–4, 12,000 clean fitting images, and the frozen clean teacher maps from v2.0 are shared. The geometry masks, paired images and digit labels serve evaluation only. No jitter, translation or synthetic symbols enter training.

## Endpoints and decision

- **Primary for this follow-up:** marked-digit accuracy on the next 10,000 images after the fitting subset in the existing seed-126 permutation of the 60,000 MNIST training images. These IDs are disjoint from both fitting and the official test set. Draw two symbols using the existing template bank and legal-placement procedure, location seed 81127 and attribute seed 91127. Previous studies have used unmarked digits from the remaining training pool. This marked evaluation was not used to choose the rule.
- **Replication:** evaluate the same newly fitted models on the original 10,000 official clean/marked test pairs with the archived symbol positions and attributes. For each fit, map its training clusters to digit IDs once after fitting and reuse that mapping everywhere.
- **Secondary:** report clean accuracy, the decrease after adding symbols, the fraction of digits whose cluster assignment changes, and how much the change in the decoder output follows the added pixels. The latter is the average per-image projection of the reconstruction change onto the input change, normalized by the squared magnitude of the input change. A perfect copy has response one.
- **Whole-image position stress:** on the new 10,000 pairs, use seed 101127 to assign one of four cardinal directions per image. Translate the entire clean/marked pair three pixels only if all ink and symbols fit. Compare the centered and translated versions on exactly these eligible IDs. Preserve the unselected IDs in the full primary endpoint.
- Report per-seed paired differences, their mean, sample SD and number positive. Use the ellipse as the new default if it beats the refitted adaptive circle on primary marked ACC in at least four of five seeds, with a positive mean on both marked sets and no more than one percentage point mean clean ACC loss on either. Otherwise retain the released method. Use the rotated comparison to interpret the contribution of orientation. Do not tune width, elongation or direction after viewing the results.
