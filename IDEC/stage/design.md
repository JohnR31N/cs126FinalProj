# At which training stage does stripe orientation influence the clusters?

This follow-up was specified after the [original study](../design.md) and before training the models below. In the original strong-stripe condition, the shared autoencoder and K-means initialization already changed cluster on 42.79% of digits when stripe orientation switched. Projecting only during fine-tuning brought the final flip rate from 41.72% to 39.50%. These observations motivate testing the training stage directly.

**Prediction.** At amplitude 0.8, projecting the reconstruction residual during autoencoder pretraining will lower the final within-image flip rate compared with ordinary pretraining when both models subsequently use projected fine-tuning. A lower flip rate immediately after pretraining would locate an effect before clustering updates. Full-pixel fine-tuning may restore some sensitivity. Digit accuracy could improve or decline, so report it alongside stability.

**Four cells.** Cross full-pixel or projected reconstruction in pretraining with full-pixel or projected reconstruction in fine-tuning. Train all four cells in this follow-up. Use the original strong-stripe data, 12,000 training and 10,000 held-out images, and all five seeds (0–4). A seed sets the same starting network and pretraining minibatch order for both pretraining losses. Fit K-means separately on each resulting embedding, score that initialization, then fork it into the two fine-tuning arms. Match optimizer settings, 100 pretraining epochs, centroids within each fork, all fine-tuning batches and 5,600 updates. The encoder sees the same striped images throughout. The projection uses the original known orientation contrast.

**Readout.** The primary contrast is the paired flip-rate difference, projected minus ordinary pretraining, with projected fine-tuning fixed. Report the same contrast at initialization and with full-pixel fine-tuning, and the difference between the two final contrasts. Average within-seed differences across the five seeds and report their sample standard deviation. Also report held-out digit ACC with the frozen training-label mapping, digit NMI/ARI and cluster–orientation NMI. Keep every cell and seed. A large drop in flips accompanied by poor digit accuracy or very few occupied clusters would not establish better digit clustering.

This is a targeted follow-up on the same split and a known synthetic pattern. Its results are separate from the original three-amplitude experiment. The four new cells provide the comparisons even if repeat runs differ from previously saved runs.
