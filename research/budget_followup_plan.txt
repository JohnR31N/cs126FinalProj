Follow-up decided after the 800-epoch CAE pilot, before any 1600-epoch runs.
Decision basis: all three CAE baselines and all three penalized CAEs failed
the paper's mean-max probability > .99 convergence criterion. The baseline
already had50 unique final features; a collision penalty was unnecessary for
that endpoint. The observed negative reconstruction result could also reflect
unequal convergence progress under an equal epoch budget.

Question: does the direction of the penalty effect persist when both methods
receive double the same budget? Train from scratch for1600 epochs; all other
configuration, seed set, data splits and evaluation procedures unchanged.
This is a declared exploratory follow-up, not the original pre-run hypothesis,
not tuning on test results, and not selection of the best final checkpoint.
Report both800 and1600 budgets with no replacement of the original results.
No claim of universal harm or novel regularization method is permitted.
